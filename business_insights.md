# Business Insights --- Supply Chain & Logistics Analytics

## Key Findings

-   **Delivery reliability needs attention.** The dashboard reports an
    on-time delivery rate of **48.81%**, indicating that fewer than half
    of recorded deliveries met their promised dates.
-   **Delivery duration is substantial.** Average delivery time is
    **16.57 days**. Reviewing delivery routes, carrier performance, and
    promised lead times may help identify opportunities to reduce
    delays.
-   **Order status is heavily concentrated in delivered orders.** The
    Order Status page shows **95.63% Delivered** and **4.37% Partially
    Shipped**. These figures describe order status, while the on-time
    delivery rate measures whether actual delivery dates met promised
    dates; they are different indicators.
-   **Shipping costs vary by carrier.** Cargo Link has the highest
    displayed total shipping cost at approximately **90K**, while Falcon
    Express is lowest at approximately **75K**. These are total costs,
    not cost-per-shipment comparisons, so volume and service mix should
    be considered before judging efficiency.
-   **Shipping method totals differ.** Standard shipping has the highest
    displayed total cost (**169K**), followed by Economy (**158K**) and
    Express (**154K**). Comparing cost per order and delivery
    performance by method would help explain these differences.
-   **Supplier lead times vary.** The Supplier Performance page shows
    lead times ranging from **9 to 21 days** among the suppliers visible
    in the chart. Suppliers with longer lead times may warrant closer
    monitoring, especially for products with limited stock.

## Recommended Actions

1.  Investigate late deliveries by region, carrier, and shipping method.
2.  Compare carriers using **cost per order**, delivery time, and
    on-time rate---not total cost alone.
3.  Review suppliers with longer lead times and consider their impact on
    replenishment planning.
4.  Track partially shipped orders and investigate recurring causes.
5.  Recheck these indicators over time and use the dashboard slicers to
    compare months, regions, categories, shipping methods, and
    suppliers.

## Interpretation Notes

-   Figures above are taken from the dashboard screenshots and reflect
    the filters shown in those screenshots. Values may change when
    slicers are applied.
-   The dashboard shows total shipping costs by carrier and method; it
    does not, by itself, establish which carrier or method is most
    cost-efficient.
-   These are descriptive findings from the displayed data, not proof of
    the causes of delays or costs.
